Material story

Every casing grows its own pattern.

The bamboo exterior of BAMBYTE One is selected for colour and grain before it is shaped. Straight fibre lines run the height of the cylinder, interrupted now and then by the darker marks of the bamboo's nodes. Those variations are not defects. They are what make each piece individual.

Bamboo forms only the exterior. Inside sit the speaker driver, battery, Bluetooth module and circuitry, protected by an internal structure that keeps them separate from the natural material.

01 · SELECT

Choosing the stock

Bamboo is graded for colour, grain consistency and visible defects before it becomes a casing.

02 · STABILISE

Treating and drying

The material is treated and conditioned to reduce moisture-related movement before machining.

03 · FINISH

Protecting the surface

A protective surface finish guards against handling, humidity and everyday marks.
