Why bamboo?

A material with character.

Bamboo is a fast-growing grass, not a tree. Its fibres run in long, straight lines, which gives a casing its fine stripes and the occasional darker mark where the stem had a node.

Natural grain patterns

No two casings are identical. The striped grain and node marks come straight from the plant.

Renewable potential

Bamboo regrows from its root system after harvesting, which makes it a promising renewable resource when it is sourced responsibly.

Warm and tactile

Bamboo feels warmer and softer to the hand than the plastic or aluminium most electronics use.
